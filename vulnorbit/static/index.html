DATA CONTRACT

Real observations, durable history

Imports retain source identities and observation times. SQLite saves the canonical catalog, source versions, and changes together. Raw GET responses are retained for 30 days with SHA-256 fingerprints.

A failed source preserves the last successful data and exposes its retry time. An empty or malformed KEV response cannot erase the catalog.

COVERAGE

A growing, bounded catalog

The full CISA KEV catalog anchors known exploitation. NVD begins with KEV metrics and seven days of published and modified CVEs, then follows both streams. GitHub starts with seven days of reviewed advisory updates.

Imports are paginated and resumable. EPSS rotates through tracked CVEs. CVE/CNA and OSV enrich records on investigation. This is not a claim to contain every historical vulnerability.

CVSS

Severity, not likelihood

A standardized technical severity score. The vector describes the conditions behind it. Different sources or CVSS versions may disagree; inspect attribution.

EPSS

Probability, not proof

FIRST estimates the probability of exploitation in the next 30 days. The dated score is predictive; a high value is not confirmation of an attack.

CISA KEV

Evidence of exploitation

KEV inclusion confirms exploitation in the wild. It does not say that your assets are affected or that an attack is happening right now.

EXPLOIT REFERENCES

Evidence needs interpretation

Source-tagged references can point to public exploit material. VulnOrbit links evidence without executing it or claiming that every reference works.
